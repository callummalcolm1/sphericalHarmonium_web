about the project

This website sonifies spectrography data from hybrid meson decompositions - listening through mass rather than time. We've attempted to sum down three dimensional energy into one dimensional sound.

the physics

The primary goal of GlueX is to search for exotic hybrid mesons: particles whose internal structure involves not just quarks, but the gluon field itself carrying quantum numbers. These states, predicted by Quantum Chromodynamics but never conclusively observed, would fundamentally deepen our understanding of how matter is bound together at the smallest scales.

The spherical harmonic moments used in this project are the mathematical tools physicists use to decode the angular distributions of GlueX decay products — the observable signatures that reveal which resonances are present.

sonification

Mesonification translates the spectroscopic fingerprints of subatomic particles — mesons — into sound. By selecting particles from the particle selector, the physics engine computes their spherical harmonic moment distributions, which are then mapped onto audio parameters in real time.

The result is a direct acoustic representation of quantum interference — the way different meson waves overlap and interact to produce the angular patterns observed in particle detectors.
